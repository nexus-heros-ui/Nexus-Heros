📖 Nexus Heroes Game Manual

Quick Start

1. Create a hero in Player Portal.
2. GM creates a world code.
3. Add hero to world, map, and player manager.
4. Generate enemies and a mission.
5. Refresh fighters and start combat.

Player Handbook

Players choose actions like Attack, Defend, Heal, Heavy Attack, or Power Attack. The GM approves the action before it happens.

🎮 Game Master Handbook

The Game Master creates the world, accepts players, controls missions, moves characters, spawns enemies, runs combat, gives rewards, and makes the final ruling when something is not covered by the game system.
Important: Wait until the website says 🟢 Multiplayer Connected before creating or joining a world.
Step 1: Open the GM Portal using the button at the top of the page.
Step 2: Enter a name in the World Name box.
Step 3: Choose Solo or Team mode using Toggle Team / Solo.
Step 4: Press 🌍 Create Multiplayer World.
Step 5: Copy the generated world code and give it to the players.

Keep the GM page open while players join. The world code is used by every player who wants to enter the campaign.

Each player must:

1. Open the same published Nexus Heroes website.
2. Create or load a hero.
3. Enter their player name and the GM's world code.
4. Press Check World.
5. Press Send Hero To GM.

The request will appear under Pending Join Requests.

Review the player's hero name, class, rank, and power score.

Press ✅ Accept to add the player or ❌ Decline to reject the request.

Accepted players begin with:

  • 100 Health
  • 100 Maximum Health
  • 100 Energy
  • 0 Shield
  • Ready status
  • No injuries
  • Nexus City starting location

Click a map location before creating missions, spawning enemies, or moving characters.

Available locations include:

  • Nexus City
  • Harbor District
  • Blackgate Prison
  • Omega Labs
  • Crash Site
  • Ancient Ruins
  • Wasteland Desert
  • Hidden Sanctuary
  • Titan Fortress
  • Dimensional Portal
  • Power Plant
  • Shadow Realm
Add Hero To Map: Adds the hero shown on the current Hero Sheet to the local map-character list.
Add Hero To Player Manager: Creates a manageable version of the hero with health, energy, shield, status, injuries, and location.
Travel Here: Moves the selected hero to the currently selected location.
Next World Turn: Advances time outside combat and may create travel or world events.
World turns and combat turns are different. World turns control travel, missions, and events. Combat turns control who acts during a battle.
Step 1: Select the map location where the mission will happen.
Step 2: Confirm the location appears under Mission Location.
Step 3: Press 📜 Generate Mission.
Step 4: Read the generated mission brief and explain the situation to the players.

The generated mission is a story prompt. The GM can change names, enemies, objectives, and details to fit the campaign.

Possible mission objectives include:

  • Defeat a villain
  • Rescue civilians
  • Capture an escaped enemy
  • Protect a location
  • Disable a machine
  • Retrieve an artifact
  • Escape a dangerous area
  • Survive a certain number of rounds

Select a map location before adding enemies. New enemies will be placed at the selected location.

Generate Villain: Creates a villain sheet with a name, species, origin, threat level, class, score, and rank.
Add Villain: Adds the generated villain to the Enemy Manager.
Add Minion: Creates a weaker enemy with lower health and energy.
Add Elite: Creates a stronger enemy that can protect a villain or lead minions.

Recommended encounters:

Small encounter
1 villain or elite
1–2 minions
Major encounter
1 villain
2 elites
2–4 minions
Boss encounter
1 powerful villain
2–3 elites
4–6 minions

Use the Enemy Manager to damage, heal, move, or remove enemies.

The Player Manager tracks:

  • Health
  • Maximum health
  • Energy
  • Shield
  • Status
  • Injuries
  • Current location

Suggested health conditions:

61–100 Health: Ready
31–60 Health: Minor Injury
1–30 Health: Serious Injury
0 Health: Knocked Out

The GM decides when a condition, injury, shield, or special effect should change.

Before starting combat, confirm that:

✅ At least one hero is in the Player Manager.
✅ At least one enemy is in the Enemy Manager.
✅ Everyone is at the correct map location.
✅ The correct mission is active.
✅ Health and energy values are correct.
Step 1: Press 🔄 Refresh Fighters.
Step 2: Confirm heroes appear in the Attacker list and enemies appear in the Target list.
Step 3: Press ▶ Start Turns.

The Turn Tracker will show the current round, current fighter, and upcoming fighters.

Step 1: Ask the player what they want their hero to do.
Step 2: Select the hero in the Attacker dropdown.
Step 3: Select the enemy or target.
Step 4: Choose the player's action.

Attack: Normal damage with normal accuracy.

Defend: Adds defensive protection or shield.

Heal: Restores health.

Heavy Attack: Higher damage with lower accuracy.

Power Attack: Strong power-based attack that may use energy.

Step 5: Press ✅ GM Approve Action.
Step 6: Read the result in the Combat Log.
Step 7: Press ⏭ End Turn.

When an enemy's turn begins, select the enemy and the hero it will target.

Press 👹 Enemy Attack for a standard enemy attack.
Press 🎲 Enemy AI Turn to allow the game to choose an action.
Read the result in the Combat Log and update the story.
Press ⏭ End Turn when the enemy is finished.

Use Reset Turns only when combat ends or the turn order needs to be completely restarted.

Combat can end when:

  • All enemies are defeated or removed
  • All heroes are knocked out
  • The heroes escape
  • The enemies surrender
  • The mission objective is completed
  • The GM decides the encounter has ended

Not every mission requires defeating every enemy. The objective may be more important than the fight.

Use Give 100 Credits to award money or test the shop.
Use Generate Loot to create a random reward.
Use Refresh Shop to generate new shop items.
Select an item and press Buy Selected Item to purchase it.

Possible rewards include:

  • Credits
  • Weapons
  • Armor
  • Artifacts
  • Consumables
  • Power upgrades
  • Reputation
  • Bonus stat points
Press 💾 Save Campaign at the end of every game session.

The campaign save stores local game information such as:

  • World players
  • Map characters
  • Managed players
  • Enemies
  • Inventory
  • Credits
  • World turn
  • Active events
  • Equipped gear
  • Combat information
Press 📂 Load Campaign when returning to a saved campaign on the same browser and device.
Reset Campaign erases the local campaign. Only use it when starting completely over.
Before the game
1. Open the website.
2. Wait for Multiplayer Connected.
3. Open the GM Portal.
4. Load the campaign or create a world.
5. Choose Team or Solo mode.
6. Select the starting location.
When players join
7. Give players the world code.
8. Accept their join requests.
9. Confirm they appear in Accepted Multiplayer Players.
10. Confirm their health and starting location.
Before a mission
11. Select a map location.
12. Generate a mission.
13. Explain the mission to the players.
14. Spawn enemies.
15. Confirm everyone is at the correct location.
During combat
16. Refresh fighters.
17. Start turns.
18. Select the attacker and target.
19. Choose and approve actions.
20. Run enemy turns.
21. End each turn.
22. Continue until the encounter ends.
After the mission
23. Remove defeated enemies.
24. Give credits and loot.
25. Update injuries and health.
26. Advance the world turn.
27. Save the campaign.

The upcoming multiplayer updates will automatically connect accepted players to the Player Manager, map markers, movement system, combat system, and turn order.

Combat

Attack = normal damage.
Heavy Attack = stronger damage but less accurate.
Power Attack = costs energy but hits harder.
Defend = adds shield.
Heal = restores health.

Inventory

Loot items can be equipped as weapons, armor, or artifacts. Gear can improve future combat systems.

Shop

Use Nexus City Shop to buy generated items with credits.

Campaign Save

Campaign Save stores players, enemies, inventory, credits, world turn, active missions, and map characters.
